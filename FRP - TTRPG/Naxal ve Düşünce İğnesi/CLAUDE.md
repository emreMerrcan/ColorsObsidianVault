## Premise

The campaign is set in **Xylorea**, a city in the Fabulous world (see `Fabulous/CLAUDE.md`). Xylorea:
- is a vast, layered, hive-like city built on an ancient buried one (Xy'Qalak's bones are its foundation)
- has an endless buried city underneath, stretching down to Zaemphea
- is ruled by competing **Razae** houses under the **Safir Efendiler** (Xea masters)
- has slaves everywhere, and authority is never questioned
- treats **RÜYA** (dream) as a person's inner passion. A strong dream makes you special, with anime / Hazbin Hotel-style larger-than-life characters.

**The spine of the plot:**
- **Discovery:** Hovoz's mines (really excavations of Xylorea's buried roots, owned by Razae Upariez) unearth the **Düşünce İğnesi / Ienaen**. It's a godlike artifact that links, reads and finally rewrites minds.
- **The theft:** Naxal, a legendary, feared fighter who worked pest control in the mines, steals it. He wants to buy his way out of the city through a Marhaz connection.
- **The Ametist Hayalet** (of the Son Işık Direnişi) catches Naxal and rewires his mind. The plan is for Naxal to destroy the Aksaağaç, and he becomes an enemy of the Razae hierarchy. The needle is hidden in the blue temple, under the Sapphire masters' noses.
- **Razae Upariez** wants the needle for a universal slavery system. They hunt Naxal and the party with the Akılsızlar.

Per `Arşiv/Ton ve Eğilimler.md`, **Naxal's story is a skeleton. The players are the focus.** Themes: being special, feeding passions, escaping death.

## Player characters

Only three PCs appear in the player contrasts in the tone note: Ajinn, Kian and Sanja. Banx is an NPC companion with hooks for each of them.

- **Sanja (18):**
  - Her parents are mine slaves whose brains were hollowed out (akılsız).
  - Her mind is cut off from Whairion, which makes her immune to psychic damage. Her detachment fits the needle.
  - She is drawn toward the Müptelalar and their drug, Ilık.
  - She has the strange tree's seed.
  - "ŞEVVAL NOTLARI" in her note suggests Şevval is her player.
- **Kian Webb:**
  - He never dreams. He's immune to effects on the soul, but material effects of spiritual events hit him double.
  - He's a math savant who counts compulsively and has a fractal madness in the Sintra style.
  - His father was beaten to death in front of him, and the sailor-accountant Durqe raised him.
  - He's writing a math book for the children (a "Principia").
- **Jish'Ajinn:**
  - Card / tını magic (Hülya, Ayrışma, Bütünleşme, Artış) and possession of corpses.
  - Her memory before the last 15 years was wiped.
  - She reads tarot.
- **Banx (NPC):**
  - Naxal's apprentice. She escaped the Suskunlar of Razae Marhaz, who raised her for a sainthood trial.
  - Her death touch destroys souls. She feels no pain or temperature and is terrified of the dark.
  - She keeps a strict no-kill rule.

## Rules system

This is the user's homebrew system (`FRP - TTRPG/SYSTEM DESIGN/`, hub: `Narrative System/System Hub.md`). This campaign plays the **legacy** version (`LEGACY SYSTEM/`: XP, d100-era SL); the new Narrative System is still being built. Its pieces:
- **Attributes:** STR / AGL / THG / INT / CHR / WP.
- **Checks:** SL (success levels), Pace P(n) per action, Endurance, Resistance, Movement, Öz/Essence, keywords and tiers.
- **XP:** 6900 at the start, aiming for about 20k by session 20. Normal sessions give 300–500, big fights +500, bosses 2–3k.
- **Party karma:** a small tracked number. It went -1 after Banx's necklace incident.

## GM style, from `Ton ve Eğilimler.md`

- Create situations, not plotlines. Everything should touch the players and also be bigger than them.
- Plan interesting outcomes for failure so losing is fun.
- In combat, everyone has a role, and the fight should turn at least once. Phase 2s add dynamism. Objective / optimizer / hazard / chaos lists are in that note.
- Notes are dense Turkish with English mixed in. Images carry NPC descriptions in their file names.
