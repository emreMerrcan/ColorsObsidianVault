This is a summary of the campaign as the notes stand (read 2026-10-21). The user is the GM. It's a map, not canon. When a detail matters, open the source note. The vault-wide rules in the root `CLAUDE.md` apply here too.

## Premise

Emenos is a rotting trade city in **southern Aldha'Menor** in the Fabulous world (see `Fabulous/CLAUDE.md`). The time is late Menor history: Taykar and Pattonia fight over Rhenes, the Gaga Klanı's crows are active, and Elruna's influence spreads. The campaign is street-level dark intrigue:
- gangs, merchant monopolies, a brothel spy network, a shadow king who never dies
- an "ash" (kül) research project that tries to beat death
- creeping horror: the Solgun Kule / Pale Palace, ziran creatures, a whispering "buyuran" god

The session log follows **one player character, Gael**:
- a "kara yürek" (dark heart)
- can only speak the truth, and is drawn to a language she half understands (Stan's diary)
- a Solgun Kimse bit off some of her fingers
- the Pale King may call her "daughter" (planned)

`Gael.md` holds one line only. Gael belongs to the player, so don't decide Gael's thoughts or actions.
