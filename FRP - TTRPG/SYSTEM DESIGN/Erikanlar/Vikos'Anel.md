# Vikos'Anel

**Crown Killers**

Size Class : Medium\~\~

|  |  |
|---|---|
| HP | ::  S+T |
| END | ::  3HP |
| RES | ::  Natural 1 |
| MOV | ::  AB |
| ESNC | :: WP+CH |
| PACE | :: 10 |

Shapeshifter : Can shapeshift into other beings.

Immune Blood : Resistant to disease and poison.

Regeneration : Burn ESNC to heal WP+Skill SL. Also healing effects are +2 stronger on you.

Jack of all Traits : Your skill limitation per level has increased by +2

Home in Wilderness : Experienced in outerwilds and in tune with nature.

Flammable : Your body is easy to catch fire. Burning effects increase by 2 in turn.

Strong Resolve : +1SL against getting persuaded, mental hardships, fear, doubt, againt shaken out of your purpose.

Attributes Evolved : Spend 8 Pace and then sacrifice 10 from one attribute to gain 10 on another.

Shape Alteration : Each alteration takes Pace. For one time use, all alterations have a specific cost. But by taking Trasnformation action, you can lower that cost for future uses.

Transformation : Change a part of your body, so you can use that alteration with -2 Pace Cost from now on. This chance costs Alteration Cost - 2 Pace. All transformations are permenant and will stay until you Transform out of them with the same cost again.

Essential Supplements : Use ESNC to reduce your Pace Cost's by 4 in a turn on alterations.

Flesh Tendrils : STRB SL grapple upto 10m

Extra Limbs : Grow whatever

Outside In : Take an object inside you.

A New Joint: Reach afar

Consume Calories : Devour and digest something instanlty, gaining ESNC from it.

Petrified Skin : Get +2 passive resistence 5P.

Thorn Shell : Inflict 3 damage to all that get in contact with you.

Bone Spike :  SL+1 , P(3), O
Grow a bone spear P5

Reaper Claws : SL, P(2), K
2SL+ inflicts 2 bleeding per instance 
Grow claws P4

Acidic Puke : 3xSL, U, acid (works like bleed) 7P

Face Swap : Chance your indentity, 3P as you hold it.
