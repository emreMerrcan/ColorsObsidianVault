# Arik'Xea

**The Jewel Masters**

Size Class : Medium

|  |  |
|---|---|
| HP | ::  S+2T |
| END | ::  HP |
| RES | ::  Natural 1 |
| MOV | :: AB |
| ESNC | :: - |
| PACE | :: 10 |

Jewel Master : Care only for youself and your species. Intense sense of tirbalism. Feel a constant and relentless urge to climb, to be crowned, and to rule over all.

Puppet Master : Control many bodies at the same time. Their Intelligence, Charisma and Will Power stats gets modifiers from yours. (channel your passive int, chr or wp through them, as much as you pass WP)

Deep Tunnel : Stream your conciousness through all your vessels. Use all your pace half your movement, but control them with max efficiency.

Inspiring : Insire youself and others effectively. Get +2SL on social checks and also while recovering END.

Crown Wings : Your crown voice commands the puppets to your will. It effects every being that has Crown Spikes within 500m. Every 100m's of distance from your puppet, their actions that come from you gets -1SL from.

Empty Shell : You have no soul. Can't interact with the secret world at all and death means death.

Attributes Evolved : Spend 8 Pace and then sacrifice 10 from one attribute to gain 10 on another.

Shape Alteration : Each alteration takes Pace. For one time use, all alterations have a specific cost. But by taking Trasnformation action, you can lower that cost for future uses.

Transformation : Change a part of your body, so you can use that alteration with -2 Pace Cost from now on. This chance costs Alteration Cost - 2 Pace. All transformations are permenant and will stay until you Transform out of them with the same cost again.

Essential Supplements : Use ESNC to reduce your Pace Cost's by 3 in a turn on alterations.

Four Armed : Can take the Ambidextrous keyword thrice.

Ambidextrous : Get +2 Pace while dual wielding with proficient weapons.

Blessed Mind : Have amplified cognitive capabilities. Logic and sciences feels natural to you.

Psychotic Overfixation: See patterns and explonations much more efficiently, sometimes even if they're not there. Combining with the Crown Jewel, you find everything to be about you.

Track & Depose : Can predict and track the actions or positions of others (INT to guess or aim ahead of time)

Eyeless Master : You see through your puppets. Other then that, your blind.
