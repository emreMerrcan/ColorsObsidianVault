
# Keywords
Keywords have 3 essencial types:
- Those which declare a profession and give advantage on related tests (roll twice, use the higher result).
- Those add direct +SL or other effects rather than declaring profession.
- Those don't do both and simply declaring a property the player has (magic system keywords or language typa stuff).

> [!claude] Claude · 2026-10-31
> 🚩 **Kategoriler (U17):** keyword kategorileri eski 4'lü: **Background / Feature / Proficiency / Others**. Yukarıdaki "3 essencial types" kategori olarak kullanılmayacak; metni sen düzelt ya da kaldır.
> - 🚩 Yine de 1. tipteki mekanik (meslek keyword'ü ilgili testlerde advantage verir) eski Background / Profession keywordlerine kural olarak geçsin mi? Eski sayfada bu keywordlerin mekaniği yok, sadece "bilirsin".
>
> **Keyword almak** (eski [[General Rulings]], milestone sistemiyle uyumlu)
> - Keywordler rol ve geçmişe sıkı bağlı. Alınıp alınamayacağına GM karar verir.
> - XP'yle alınmaz, eşikle gelir: yeni sistemde milestone'daki "+Keyword" adımı ([[Progression]]).
> - Proficiency keywordleri bol verilebilir. Oyun tarzını belirleyenler (çoğu Others) idareli: karakter başına bir iki tane.
> - Oyuncu kendi isteğiyle **negatif** bir keyword alırsa slot yemeyebilir.
> - Başlangıç: 2 Background, 2 Feature, 2 Proficiency. Others ırk / Class / GM'den. Irk keywordleri sayılmaz ([[Progression]]).
> - 🚩 Eski "4 Background, 2 Feature, 1 Combat" oranı: Combat diye kategori yok, başlangıç da artık 2-2-2. Tavsiye olarak yaşasın mı?
>
> Eski keywordlerin hepsi, yeni sisteme çevrilmiş halleriyle: aşağıda, [[#Help]]'in hemen üstünde.

### Birthplace:
#### Menora (Rhenes, Patton): 
Sunblessed: Have +1 END under direct sunlight or haron, and -1 without it.
Gold Weigher: Keen eye for evaluating metals.
Strong Resolve, Agonist, +1 Half Action proficiency, +1 Melee weapon proficiency
Sacrifical Dedication : Can get +SL to your dice or straight up accomplish something with grave consequences (rule based, add WPB to the next roll with instant half health lost)

> [!claude] Claude · 2026-10-31
> Menora'daki açıklamasız keywordlerin eski tanımları ([[Keywords]]):
> - **Strong Resolve:** ikna edilmeye, zihinsel zorluklara, korkuya, şüpheye, amacından sarsılmaya karşı +1SL.
> - **Agonist:** acıya dayanıklı. END'e +WPB.

> - **+1 Melee weapon proficiency** ↔ eski **Proficient Weapon (any)**: belirli bir silah tipinde -SL almazsın.

# Help
Players or npcs can use reactions to protect or aid each other, or unite their turns to take simultenous actions.

AID TYPES

1. One action helping anothers:
I help therefore hes easier = advantage on the second person. We join efforts for a single outcome = SL's gets added together,
One player grappling the enemy so the other can hit easily. One rolls STR+ATL and grapples. His outcome, gives a major advantage, maybe directly added to the attack of their ally, but the first action must pass on its own first.

2. Two joined actions 
Opposing against a single action in unison = SL's gets added together WITH a 1-3 syncronization difficulty added after the addition, 
Two knights wrestling with a troll. They both roll STR + ATL and join their SL to extend their SL roof.

3. Dividing an action for group handling
I have this part and you do have that, so the stats join for a single roll. Lets say theres a big ballista and its used with STR+Accuracy and one strong player and one accurate player standing nearby. They both handle it, one gives their STR and the other gives Accuracy to shoot it.
