
## Theory

At the end you're not a god but a pretty dynamic qualified person.

For a full character:
+5+3+1 STAT and 8-8-8 KEY and 5+5+4+4+3+3+2+2+1 SKILL
9 stat, 24 key, 29 skill    AT FULL should look like this
+4 STAT, +6 Keyword, +9 Skill      subtract these from the startup
+5 STAT, +18 keyword, 20 Skill     should come from milestones

Per milestone
1 Stat, 3 keyword (+3 sprinkled), 4 skill

Starting:
STATS: At the origin of -5, have one +2, two +1, two -1, one -2 stat
SKILLS: Have 1x3, 2x2's and 2x1s
Keyword: 6 keywords, 2-2-2

## Milestone Schematic
### Progress Pace
At total there's 44 milestones. GM's should plan for the length and the progress that will take place within that time.
PER TIER : 8 milestones in Y sessions
HALFWAY : 26 milestones in Y sessions
COMPLETE PROGRESS : 44 milestones in Y sessions

- Lifelong Epic: Give 1 milestone every 3 sessions
PER TIER : 8 milestones in 24 sessions
HALFWAY : 26 milestones in 78 sessions
COMPLETE PROGRESS : 44 milestones in 132 sessions

- Long Campaign: Can give 1 milestone for each session
PER TIER : 8 milestones in 8 sessions
HALFWAY : 26 milestones in 26 sessions
COMPLETE PROGRESS : 44 milestones in 44 sessions

- Medium Adventure: Can give 1 and on occasions give 2 milestone each session
PER TIER : 8 milestones in 6 sessions
HALFWAY : 26 milestones in 18 sessions
COMPLETE PROGRESS : 44 milestones in 30 sessions

- Short Quest: Can give 2 every session
PER TIER : 8 milestones in 4 sessions
HALFWAY : 26 milestones in 13 sessions
COMPLETE PROGRESS : 44 milestones in 22 sessions

- Time Jumping: Give 4 every session
PER TIER : 8 milestones in 2 sessions
HALFWAY : 26 milestones in 7 sessions
COMPLETE PROGRESS : 44 milestones in 11 sessions
