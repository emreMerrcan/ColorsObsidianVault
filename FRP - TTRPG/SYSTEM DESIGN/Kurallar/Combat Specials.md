# COMBAT SPECIALS

A player can use any weapong creatively. They may ask to cut multiple perfectly lined targets with a kitchen knife with one swipe or they hit someone on the head with the handle of their rifle. A knife becomes an area of effect weapon and a sniper turns into a hammer that bonks heads. Any kind of use can be made up along the way but heres some example moves that players might want to regularly use and see the effect of.

## Half Actions/Moves

Moves count as half actions, can be taken without spending an actual action or getting penalty. If you do 2 of these, they become one full action. You can make up your own move, stat checks and the effects depend on your creativity and circumstances. 

Opportunity Reaction : When a person is moving through your range of reaction (1m) you can use a reaction move on. For example, you can trip them, grapple, shove, stun the enemy,  basically any move that is a direct reaction against them.

* Block: Resist an attack with a part of armour or object. Roll STR+physical check and oppose the upcoming attack. If you pass, then apply the objects resistence to the upcoming attack. Weapons block as much as their PACE - 1. ALSO a shields can have its own Pace Cost.

* Soak: Roll THG+FORT to shrug SL amount of damage from registering to your health. On hit effects still apply.

* Dodge: Roll AGL+Physical Check and evade an attack. Reduce your SL from enemy attack SL, mitigating their aim.

* Dash: Extend your movement by Mov/2 amount of meters.

* Jump: Leap forward with STRB/2 or AGLB/2 meters length and height.

* Shove: Roll STR+ATL to toss something aside for SL meters (depends on mass diffrence)

* Grapple or Snare: Hold something motionless, passing their Passive STR. Give +20 to those that target them. Opponent must pass your SL on opposition to get free.

* Hit to Stun: Get 2SL reduction on Daze threshold of your weapon. 

* Aim: Sacrifice movement to get +1SL . Also+2SL more accuracy for manual targeting

* Shout: CHR+Expr/Dcp, Apply social effects like, intimidate, startle or fake intention to get advantage. Must pass Passive CHR or contest.

* Aid: Help another, lending +SL by sharing your advantages over the action (+1SL base)

* Analysis: Roll to analyse the field/situtation/enemy with intelligence or charisma.

* Ready Action: Declare an action and hold it. When you release, it happens with priority despite initiative order, cutting your enemy off, also get +1SL.

* Lock In: Get +1SL on an action by keeping momentary focus on it.

* Counter Diversion : Roll for attack with your weapon against upcoming attack, enemy SL is hindered by your SL

* Counter Block : Roll for attack with your weapon against upcoming attack, on success, add weapon RES over your armor

## Weapon Specials

Weapons have special effects and additions on standard actions. They can be used situationally. Not every special can result favourably. For example, a sword can be used to make a dazing attack by hitting its handle, but if the palyer attempts such move against a ready enemy they will get a big disadvantage on the opposed test. They are a reward system for roleplaying in combat more then actual skills to throw in fights.

> Aşağıdaki iki layer **sayfanın dışında**: Stances metni sol kenarın tamamen dışında (x=-918) ve çok koyu renkte (#353535), yan yatırılmış "SL opposing…" etiketi de yarısı dışarıda. PDF'te görünmüyorlar; .pxz'de duruyorlar.

Stances are a way of demonstrating what your character prioritize on their movements. If you are staying safe like a turtle and wont extend too much on your attacks, you're taking the defencive stance. A player can make up their own stances if the DM permits.

Ready Action: Get a +1 SL to the action you prepare. You can enact on it to interrupt an enemy or just use it next turn.

All-Out Stance: Get +2SL on your offense but also give +2SL to all opponent attacks

Defencive Stance: Get +3 resistence but also give +2SL  to all opponent attacks

Evasive Stance: Give -2SL to all opposing attacks but also take +SL damage from them

Dual Wielding: With ambidextrous & proficiency, hold two weapons to get +10 to extra actions

SL opposing       statstick          statstick+threshold
