# KEYWORDS

## Background Keywords

Background (any) : Have ties to a background (example, Noble Blood, Kingpin, Watermen, Tradesman...)

Profession (any) : Have proficiency in a dicipline (example. Thievery, Sailor, Fashion Master, Make Up Artist, Dealmaker, Herbalist...)

Language (any) : Know the specified language (example, Menorian, Zaemphean Tounges, Sintrian Tounges...)

Culture (any) : Familiar with a culture (example, Nations cultures like Menorian, Caliberrian, Xylorean, Chelissan, or regions like the Purple Isles, The Mainland, the new world)

Religion (any) : Knows the rituals and laws of a religion (example, Araad of the Golden Sun, Apostasy of the Mother Crow, Folk Ritual Wisdom, Secrecy Wisdom, Tayhun Elder Myths...)

Read/Write : Know to read and write.

Urban Cat : Experienced of life in cities and the ways of back alleys.

Home in Wilderness : Experienced of outerwilds and knowledge of nature

Noble Blood: Familiar of noble culture and etuqiette

Tradesman : Experienced in trading culture and methods

Thief : Experienced in culture of thievery and acts of petty crime

Kingpin : Experienced in crime culture and hierarchies

Cook : Make considerably well meals

Drive : Know how to drive carts or vehicles.

Sail : Have experience on sailing.

Seaways : Know the cultures and nations of the far seas.

Watermen : Can read the waves and skies easily. Life in seas are known to you.

Wealthy : Have +50 gold

Alcoholic : You can outdrink your opponents

Artistic : Proficient in a brand of art like music, painting or sculpting.

Herbalist : Make potions and utulize nature.

Surgeon : Needles and razors, you can oporate and heal.

History Major : Educated in a branch of history.

Make Up Artist : Know the art of beauty, making hair and body paints

Fashion Master : Know fashion, its culture and evaluation.

Dealmaker : Good at haggling and making people agree with your terms.

Scale Sheer Surface : Can traverse almost any surface.

Track and Trap : Can track and make traps.

Public Figure : Can effectively talk to a crowd.

False Identity : Can act like others, make accents, mimic cultures

Orientation : Always have sense of where you are.

Evaluate Price: Guess somethings value

## Feature Keywords

Attractive : Youre appealing to people (+1 charm)

Muscular Body : Have a muscular body. Also get +2 damage on all strength damages.

Giant : Have huge body, incredible strength and mass. +4 on STR based damages.

Night Vision : Can see in almost pitch black.

Tireless : Have incredible stamina. Regen 3 END by every minute you're out of combat till half of your total END.

Sprinter : Get +1 Mov

Four Armed: Can get ambidextrous keyword thrice

Soft Tissue : You are vulnerable (-10) against physical damage

Albino : Sun or strong effects over your skin hurts you

Iron Mantle: +1 natural Resistence

Hard Bones : +TB to ONLY Health

Tolerant Stance : +TB to ONLY Endurance

Agonist : Resistant to pain. +WPB to endurance.

Hollow Mind : Invincible to psychology.

Blessed Mind : Have amplified cognitive capabilities

Strong Resolve : +1SL against getting persuaded, mental hardships, fear, doubt, against getting shaken out of your purpose

Cold Tempered : You can keep calm in dire situtations.

Insomniac : Start everyday with 1 exhaustion minimum. Forced sleep can negate this.

Night Owl : A sleepless life leaves you under- developed but gives more time for studying. Treat all your attributes as if they were +10 higher while spending XP but get +1 keyword for every two keywords you have. 

Present Spirit: Spells and mystic effects on you gets applied as if they had -1SL to the minimum of 1SL

Indomitable Spirit : You get advantage when the odds are stacked against you and against daze, stun, breake spirit kind of attacks.

Psychcotic Overfixation : See patterns and explonations much efficiently, sometimes even when they're not there.

Terrifying : People dont wanna mess with you and feel unease.

Animal Affinity : Animals feel comfort with you.

Adaptive Mimic : Learn new concepts and behaviours  more easily.

Lifelong Variety : Pick 2 additional keywords

Odly Facade : An abberation of your kind, maybe by mutation or corruption

Mental Condition : Difference on your psychology, an ability or a disability

Falling Cat: Your damageless fall length is doubled

Far Leap : Can jump double the length and height

Persuater : Get +1SL on twisting others intentions

Intimidating : Get +1SL on crushing others intenions

Deceitful : Get +1SL on shrouding the truth

Sharp Reflexes : +1 Passive AGL against unforseen

Ready Eye : +1 Passive Persception

Stiff Blood : +1 Passive THG to poisons and sickness

Knowledge Seeker: ESNC can be Keyword count / 2 at minimum

Mistral Channeling: If WP is not your highest attribute get +2ESNC

Evasive Steps: Add your AGP to your res, counts only against targeted actions (not AoE, homing or direct effects)

Immovable: Add THP to res

## Proficiency Keywords

Proficient Weapon (any) : Don't get -SL on a specific type of weapon

Move Master (any) : Get +1SL on a specific type of Half Action

Ambidextrous : Get +2 Pace while dual wielding with proficient weapons

Brawling Menace : Get +1SL on dirty fighting moves and with improvised weapons

Combat Analyst : Can spot weak spots of enemies you're unfamiliar with.

Fencer : Effectively target specific points. Get +10 on Manual Hit Location attacks.

Sharpshooter : Aim also gives -2SL to the enemies trying to evade your shots.

Sniper : Aim lets you shoot upto one tier above your range limit.

Action Shooter : No disadvantage on shooting while moving/riding, reduced in ectreme cases.

Furious Assault : +2 Pace

Team Player : Get +1SL while aiding others on their actions.

Fall Taker : Know how to get hit or fall. As long as you can move, get 1 passive resistance, -4 fall damage and get +1SL while dodging

Champion : After a successful defence, your next attack gets +2SL advantage.

Retaliate : After a Block, Dodge or any defencive action, get +2 Pace (per turn). 

Shell Piercer : With a proficient weapon, lower the enemy Weakpoint Threshold by 2SL.

Combat Weteran : Disadvantages against you and advantages of you based on tactics are amplified. Know how to retreat and flank effectively.

Berserker : Reduced disadvantage on negative health and also of injurises.

Butcher : Attacks higher then 10SL inflict double base damage

Charger : Move on a straight line to attack and get +SL on it

Double Step : Second time you take the same half action, penalty wont apply

Many Gestures : Second time you take a different half action, penalty wont apply

Instant Impact : On opposition, your actions happen before your enemies, having the power to effect or cut off theirs.

Track & Depose : Can predict and track the actions or positions of others (mostly INT)  (position tracking for ranged too)

Point of Success : Rolls between 1-10 also counts as critical, and Manual Critical Attack threshold reduced by 1

Decapitate : Critical hits, passing the armor, auto Chop's or rolls on Critical Wounds

Throat Cutter : If your critical damage was equal to the enemies health left on a health pool (like END, HP or -HP), reduce it to 0.

Freerunning : Get +1SL while climbing and traversing with speed

Concealing Shadow : Returning to stealth or losing track, +1SL

Evasive Dash: While evading, also dash Mov amount of distance

Hardened Posture: Get +2 more soak on your Soak action.

Death from Above : Get no disadvantage on aerial combat and crit with your 5SL+ actions while falling onto an enemy

False Calls : +SL on using deception in combat

Sentinel : React when an ally near you is effected

Dashing Strike : Dash while attacking. Add +1 Pace and MOV or STB amount of dmg to your attack.

Momentum Actions : Action with 6SL+ gives a free half action per turn

Counter Master : On blocking with your weapon, beat DN with 6SL+ to get a free bonus action next turn

Combat Dexterity : Switch, prepare or reload weapons swiftly, 2 free Pace for reloads

Heavy Weaponry : Get +2 Pace once in turn when you spend pace on a weapon with 6 or more cost

Core Arsenal : Get +2 Pace once in turn when spending pace on  a weapon with 3 - 6 pace.

Swift in Arms :  Get +2 Pace once in turn when you spend pace on a weapn with 3 or less cost

Outpacing Predator : Extra Movement actions take 7 Pace instead of all of it

Battle Frenzy : When endurance is drained, get +2 to Pace.

Brief Composure : If you have Endurance, get +2 Pace

Faster Punch: Your first SB amount of punches only cost 1 pace

Heavier Punch: Fists get +1 Pace but instead of basing damage on SB, they deal SL + whatever

Berserker : 2SL reduced disadvantage on negative health and also of injurises.

Death Wish : +2SL on low health saving throws

Unyielding : Can roll to snap out of Stuns, passing a THB-5 SL test

Last Breath : On death, get +1 HP instead.

Shivering Words: Social brutality, your speeches gain +1  effectiveness (1 longer daze, +1 advantage or -1 disadvantage)

Inspiring : Inspire yourself and your team in dire situations (social skill). Also get +2SL while recovering END with morale.
