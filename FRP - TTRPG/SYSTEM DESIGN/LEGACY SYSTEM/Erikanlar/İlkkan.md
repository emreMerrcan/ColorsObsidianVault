# First Kin

**Artisans of the Secret World**

Size Class : Large

|  |  |
|---|---|
| HP | ::  2T+2W |
| END | ::  HP |
| RES | ::  - |
| MOV | ::  AB |
| ESNC | ::  WP+Foc+3 |
| PACE | ::  10 |

Four Armed : Can get ambidextrous keyword thrice.

Asecend and Rebirth: You fulfill the experience of this mask. To a new dance with a new face, you let go of your older self, becoming someone anew with all the memories of before. Store your old self in a mask to use Kindled Faces.

Kindled Faces : Spend all essence to drop your current self. Be plain as the void. Then spend all essence to regain yourself as one of your masks. The mask gives you a new personality, skills and keywords.

Spirit Rebound : When you die, if your soul havent been killed yet, you take a premature ascension. Wiping the personality, skills and attributes of your character clean, and reforming your flesh body as if they're someone new, with some lingering memories of what happened before. You also start with 1 Essence and it recovers overtime with mental struggle on your newly found conciousness.

Mesmerizing Domain : Your very gestures can change how this world appears to others.

True Sight : See the secret world and its residents. See the flow of essence and the gestures of the world. (higher dimensions of sight need Scholar)

Consume Flesh : Keep a carnivorous diet for bodily healing.

Sustanance Meditation : Refill your END and ESNC through a couple hours of singing and dancing.

Sacret Geometry : Can see patterns and secret gestures of the world around you. 

Self Absorbed : Extremely bad at learning from others. Develop your very own way of doing things suits you more.

Albino : Sun and some strong effects over your skin burns you.

Soft Tissue : Physical damage hurts your tender skin a lot more.
