# Azothan

> [!claude] Claude · 2026-10-24
> **1. versiyon:** Irklar I (`annie-spratt-6a3nqQ1YwBw-unsplash4.pxz`, PDF `…unsplashzz.pdf`).

**Bone Bursters**

Size Class : Medium

|  |  |
|---|---|
| HP | :: S+2T |
| END | :: Puppet HP |
| RES | ::  Natural 1 |
| MOV | ::  AB+1 |
| ESNC | ::  WP+Foc+2 |
| PACE | ::  10 |

Tireless : Have incredible stamina. Regen 3 END by every minute you're out of combat untill you heal half of your total END.

Sprinter : Get +1 Mov

Marrow Walker : If you get contact on the skin of another being, pierce it to posess its bone marrow. Spend you enemies Health Points amount of Pace to totally ifiltrate their body. The rate of transition is up to you, and you can continue after an interruption. Afterwards, the puppet immediately gains immunity to bleeding and heals THGB helath back. If you posses them unaware and keep them alive, you can simply finish them off at any moment.

Flesh Puppet : Control the body of another being. Replacing your Strength, Thoughness, Agility, Movement, Resistence and Pace with theirs. Their health becomes your health and endurance BUT your negative health remains yours. Until the puppet loses its bodily functions, you can feed and regenerate it fit for its digestive needs. At a certain point, the body dies and cant regenerate. You still can move the body for a while until it completely falls apart.

Skeletal Posession : If the being is unaware of your intrusion or simply opposing your control, compell it to act however you like by taking an extra half action to overwhelm their will. Roll STR/WP + Focus/Fortitude/Athletics to pass their Passive STR or WP. If you pass with 4+ SL, you can act freely for two extra turns.

Songs of Enchantment :

Home in Wilderness : 

Exposed Core : 

Weak Body : Magnitude of your strength is not much. If you roll the same SL with a being that doesnt have this, it counts as if they won.

Claws : SB
P(3), Grapple (STRB), Bleed (2)

2x Falling Cat : Your damageless fall length is doubled.
