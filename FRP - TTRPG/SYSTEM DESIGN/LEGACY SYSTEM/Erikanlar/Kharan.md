# Kharan

**Seers of the Skies**

Size Class : Medium

:: S+2T+W
::  HP
::  Natural 1
::  AB
::  WP+Foc+1
::  10

Oirentation : Always have a sense of where you are and which direction you're facing.

Take Flight : Soar to the skies. Flying in combat and flying to travel use stamina differently.  With extreme manuevers, fly for MOV amount of minutes before resting for a minute. You can travel for a MOV amount of hours in a day, before resting for an hour.

Blessed Mind : Have enhanced cognitive capabilities. Make reason and play with numbers much more efficiently.

Sand Speaker (Kharan): Recall names and memories that are not your own. Roll for INT+Focus for such recalls.

Each Moment Recalled : Have a detalied memory of each detail on your past. Although you can forget mundane things, a name, a number, a face, a moment important to you would never fade away.

Hollow Bones : Your Passive THG is reduced by -2. Weak bones can easily break.

Weak Body : Magnitude of your strength is not much. If you roll the same SL with a being that doesnt have this, it counts as if they won.

Eyesight Wide : Can easily track things on your periferal vision or far away.

Far Leap : Double the jump length and height.

2x Falling Cat : Your damageless fall length is doubled.

Claws : SB
P(3), Grapple (STRB), Bleed (2)

Dive: A unique movement action with 3 Pace cost. Use the speed of your wings to dive onto an enemy, commiting yourself to the act. Get +2SL on other attacks and actions related to the dive.

Beak Bite : STRB+2
Piercing 2. Can tear armour and flesh.
