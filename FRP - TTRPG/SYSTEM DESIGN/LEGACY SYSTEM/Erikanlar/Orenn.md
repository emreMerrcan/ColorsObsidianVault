# Oren

**War Giants of Targ'Ens**

Size Class : Giant

|  |  |
|---|---|
| HP | ::  S+3T+W |
| END | ::  HP |
| RES | ::  Natural 3 |
| MOV | ::  2AB |
| ESNC | :: WP+FOR+2 |
| PACE | :: 10 |

Giant : Body in gigantic proportions. Have incredible strength and mass. +4 on all STR related damages.

Brutal Proportions : Your attacks sweep through an area, giving every weapon you use +5 Sweeping by default. Also due to your size, enemies smaller than you have a much easier time blocking or dodging your attacks, depending on the size class diffrence.

Four Armed : Have multiple limbs. Can get ambidextrous Keyword thrice.

Terrifying : People feel unease around you.

War Heart : Body fueled by essence through your war heart. All flesh around holds on to it, feeds from it. If it gets hurt, you die. If it cant feed your flesh, your limbs start dissolving, eventually opening your chest.

Refill Flesh : Spend Essence to roll for healing.

War Heart Rampant : Activating this gives  you +2 Pace and +1SL to your dominant stat rolls until you run out of essence.

Divine Eater : Sustain and regenerate by the flesh of alive and concious beings. Inflict extreme mental effects to heal yourself (heal the amount of the CHR+INT+WP of your enemy). Restore Essence from stealing it from eaten enemies souls.

Scorched Minds : Recall feats from those you consumed recently. Keep 1 mental Keyword (like knowledge, proficiency, tendency) from an enemy you consumed for a day.

Howling Terror : Howl your siren voice by spending 1 essence, terrorizing those around you. Spend Essence to amplify the effect. All those hear it must pass a (E\*-SL) WP check or get terrified and take E\*WPB damage.
