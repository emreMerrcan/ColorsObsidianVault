# Bai

**Dwellers of the Deep Dark**

Size Class : Small

|  |  |
|---|---|
| STR | :: S+T+W |
| THG | :: 2HP |
| AGL | ::  - |
| INT | ::  AB-1 |
| CHR | ::  WP+Foc+2 |
| WILL | ::  8+AGL |

Scale Sheer Surface : Can trevarse almost any surface. Crawl on ceilings and hide in impossible corners.

Stick Fingers : Get an excelent grip on anything you hold on to. +1SL on grapples.

Night Vision : You can see clearly in darkness.

Albino : Sun and some strong effects over your skin burns you.

Extreme Temper : Emotions takes hold easily. Your character can spiral into blind rages, deep sorrows or blissful hapiness, and may need to roll for checks to get a hold of themselves. 

Subject to Power : Strength and intimidation triggers an obedient reaction. -1SL to such rolls.

Adaptive Mimic : Learn new concepts and behaviours rapidly.

Far Leap : Cam jump double the length and height.

Weak Body : Magnitude of your strength is not much. If you roll the same SL with a being that doesnt have this, it counts as if they won.

Bone Armour : When in need, can grow bones and horns at will from anywhere on your body, sacrificing health and blood. Standard damage for such tools mimic their metal counterparts, with only 2 less base damage. Health sacrifice example table:

Horns: -1
Spiky skin: -2
 Huge fangs/claws: -3
A spear bone: -4
Partial Bone Armor: -3
Full Exosceleton: -10
Grow out daggers: -3

Bones start dissolving after a day. Bone resistence is 2. Standard damage for bone weapons mimic their metal counterparts, with only 2 less base damage. Can grow out dagger shaped bones and use as weapons, demands a level of mastery tho. Grown bones dont include marrow.
