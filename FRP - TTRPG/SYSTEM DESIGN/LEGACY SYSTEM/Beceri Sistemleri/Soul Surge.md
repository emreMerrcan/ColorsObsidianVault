# SOUL SURGE

Soul Surging uses the substence of the soul, the Essence. After spending it, to refill it all, you must take a long rest. A short rest will replenish only half of your capacity. Eating and sleeping takes importance in that matter.  Essence Proficiency is the number of your natural maximum essence. And if you run out of essence, you collapse. If, somehow, you go into negative, you get d10+2 draining damage for each negative essence.

BENCE: soulsurge ü bir attributea attuneluyorsun ve onu THG, INT, CHR gibi statlarla da atabiliyorsun. Skill muhtemelen her zaman FOCUS olacak ancak temel stat değişkenlik gösterebilir.  (amk WP + Focus sadece ESNC yükseltiyosa ilerleme nasıl olcak o zaman)

HOT TAKE: Focus u esans arttırma yanından ayır. Sadece WP arttırsın. Sonra da tüm magic sistemlerinin eğrisi düzeliyor bence. Çünkü şu an 10k xp ile falan rahat 10 esans karakter açıp OMEGA MAGE abilitiler atabiliyosun

### Body Heat

Make your body and breath warmer, giving you the ability to withstand cold environments

## 1E

### Candling Fingers

Heat focuses on your fingertips lits as a fire as big as a candles.

### Dancing Lights

Flashing lights stream out. They can take telligable shapes or colors. It lits as much as a toch light.

MILESTONE 
Can feed other essence sources, increasing their magnitude of effect.

## 2E

### Burning Hands

Your hand engulfed in flames, burning everything they touch and can melt by keeping contact.

Each turn its pressed, it deals d6+2 damage and inflicts 3 burning

### Enlightening Rays

You can make an object glow with your touch. It becomes a light source for a while depending on its size.
Also you can shoot out light rays that blocks vision and can also enilghten objects.

### Disorienting Screech

You push the essence in disorienting vibration waves. Solids are effected more.
Targets gets shaken, taking 2 turns of daze. And if the target has solid properties, like wearing armor or made of concrete substence, get d6 damage.

### Shocking Grasp

You focus essence on a point and drawing from a target, creating voltage between you and them, making electricity zap between!

Target takes d10+2 damage. They also take 2 turns of daze upon failing a THG test (stun if -3 SL)

MILESTONE
Can sense essence sources with Investigation or similiar rolls. Accuracy matches your essence proficiency level.

## 3E

### Fireball Volley

You shoot balls of fire from your hands. Each fireball can be aimed and dodged seperately.

You can shoot three seperate fireballs, each dealing d10 damage on impact, and inflicts 3 burning.

### Blinding Beacon

An eye burning light glows from you.

Everybody that was looking at you, if fail an AGL reflex test, takes SL damage and SL turns of blindess.

### Shattering Touch

An object violently shakes with a thundering inpact on touch. Solids breake while soft tissues are uneffected.

Solids take 12 flat damage. The damage echoes through connected solids.

### Soul Discharge

You focus essence on a point and drawing from a target, creating voltage between you and them, making electricity zap between!

Target takes d10+2 damage. They also take 2 turns of daze upon failing a THG test (stun if -3 SL)

MILESTONE
Can form you essence outside your body, not necceceraly having to shoot it from yourself. Also can reach other distant essence sources.

## 4E

### Bursting Flames

You shoot a constant stream of fire from your hands for the entire turn into cone shaped zone.

Deals d10 damage to each target, inflicting 8 burning.

### Purification by Haron

You or an object you enlighten shines with the burning light of the sun.

All takes d10 damage. If they fail to look away, AGL test, they take +5 damage and  5 turns of blindness.

### Thundering Impact

On a circular area 5m's wide, a shattering impact is deployed.

All effected takes 6 damage regardless. If solid, takes d10+6 damage.

### Chain Lightning

Sudden stream of essence zaps between targets.

Deals d10+5 damage and also zaps to the closest essence cloud, dealing -5 of the initial damage, jumping until it runs out. Each target take 2 turns of stunned unless they pass 2DN THG test. (stun if -4 SL)

### Explosive Contact

Essence explodes out into shock and fiery sparks. 

Deals d6+8 to the touched target and pushes them 5 meters depending on their size. They can resist, reducing push distance by their saving roll SL.

MILESTONE
You can rule the essence with force. You can spend more then your capactiy, instead of taking damage right away, you can choose to take 2 Exhaustion at the first -2 essence overcast.

## 5E

### Heat Core

A concentrated point of extreme heat, burns and spreads like a wildfire, consuming all essence.

5m's around the Heat Core, all takes 8 burning. And as long as the core exists, the burning amplifies twice as fast. Needs concentration checks to maintain the core.

### Sun Beam

Light consentrated to an evaporating beam.

Shoot it thorugh your line of sight and deal ExtraEssence \* d6+8 damage. Blind the target permenantly.

### Shattering Waves

In a cone shaped area, all solids crack and all soft object get unbalanced.

Dealing 8 damage regardless, d10+10 if solid. All solids that effected by the wave, vibrates and emits the shattering in a chain reaction. Vibrations jump to a target in 5m radius.

### Paralysing Stream

Essence steams off of your enemies soul, filling yours. 

Deal d6+5 damage and zap to closest essence cloud within 5m with -3 of initial damage each time. They all roll a 2DN THG test and if they fail they get dazed, if -2SL get stunned, if -4SL, they lose 1 essence and you get 1.

### Chrushing Wind

An explosion directed to a distance up to 10m.

Dealing d6+8 to each target within and pushing them 10 meters. They can resist to save push distance by their saving roll SL.

MILESTONE
By concentrating for 2 turns, you can create a low pressure zone around you, making the fluid essence sources easier to magnify and control. Within this field, every spell takes -1 Essence to use and you can bend and control them more efficiently.

kahar fashion forming organsm without marrow or real organic substence

Who gets caught in it, rolls -3SL daze, -4SL stun roll against getting stunned.

### Devoruring Stream

Point of low tension, drains all.

Deal d6 +(Extra Essence \* d6) +6 damage and zap to closest essence cloud within 10m with -3 of initial damage each time. They all roll a 3DN THG test and if they fail they get dazed, if -2SL get stunned, if -4SL, they lose 1 essence and you get 1.

### On Point Blast
