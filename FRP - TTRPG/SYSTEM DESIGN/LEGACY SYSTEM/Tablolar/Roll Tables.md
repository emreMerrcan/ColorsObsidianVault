# ROLL TABLES

## Mental Conditions

|  |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| 1 -10 | Hyperempathy | Feels the pain and joy of others almost physically. Might avoid fights, or break down in combat. |
| 11-20 | Compulsive Ritualism | Must perform specific rituals to feel "safe" (e.g., tracing runes before casting spells, muttering names before sleeping). |
| 21-30 | Delusional Hero Complex | Believes they are destined to save the world. May take reckless risks or interpret every encounter as fate. |
| 31-40 | Dream Anchor | Confuses dreams and reality. May receive clues or omens in dreams that they take too seriously. |
| 41-50 | Mythomania | Lies compulsively, often creating dramatic or grandiose tales. May not know what’s true anymore. |
| 51-60 | Dissociative Combat Mode | In combat, becomes distant and eerily calm, like someone else is fighting for them. Has no memory afterward. |
| 61-70 | Cryptomnesia | Believes they’re having original ideas, but is unknowingly remembering things from past lives or books read. May mix fantasy with reality. |
| 71-80 | Moral Synesthesia | Experiences morality as sensory phenomena (e.g., lies taste bitter). Has strange reactions to evil or good acts. |
| 81-85 | Phantom Voices (Non-malevolent) | Hears a helpful but cryptic voice that gives guidance or riddles. It might even be right—or not. |
| 86-90 | Paranoia of the Mundane | Distrusts everyday objects (clocks, mirrors, cutlery), claiming they’re tools of some hidden power. |

## Severe Mental Conditions

|  |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| 1 -10 | Guilt-Induced Hallucinations | Haunted by the ghosts of those who you killed or wronged—even if they were justified. These "ghosts" speak. |
| 11-20 | Blackout Rage Syndrome | Occasionally loses all control in high-stress situations. Comes to with blood on hands. |
| 21-30 | Obsessional Loyalty | Becomes unhealthily devoted to the first person who shows them genuine kindness. |
| 31-40 | Selective Amnesia | Forgets certain memories after traumatic events. These memories resurface unpredictably, causing flashbacks. |
| 41-50 | Schizo-Affinity for Symbols | Believes certain shapes or numbers are speaking to them, guiding them toward some mysterious truth. |
| 51-60 | Cannibalistic Impulse Suppression | Suppresses disturbing hunger urges. May lose control under duress. Could be tied to a curse or pact. |
| 61-70 | Empathic Predator | Feels others' emotions not to understand—but to exploit. Derives pleasure from emotional manipulation. |
| 71-80 | Masochistic Invulnerability Delusion | Genuinely believes pain is holy or empowering. Walks into traps willingly. Might gain temporary boons through suffering. |
| 81-90 | Divine Host Syndrome | Believes they are the vessel of a god—or actually are. Will act with divine entitlement and may develop powers accordingly. |
| 91-100 | Split Alignment Manifestation | Has two conflicting souls inside—one good, one evil. They argue, shift dominance, and even wrest control of the body during stress. |
