## OPENING YOUR CHARACTER 

Opening a character, you should first pick your species. Then roll for your stats according to its the addions. You can place the rolled numbers on which stat you like. 

If you have a solid background and character in mind, take the keywords talking with the GM. If you want to, you can roll for them to get extra starting XP. Eigher way, you can only take the amount specified on your starting tier at this stage. 

Then you will be given XP and Wealth depending on your tier. Spend the XP to increase your skills, stats or buy additional keywords. You can spend your gold to get starting equipment. Or if you choose so, start naked and have that amount of gold on you. 

For progression expectation, you should be getting +1000xp every 5-10 sessions depending on your game tempo and campaign tone. Spend the XP on skills or Attributes however you like, but for the Keywords, you must reach the tier threshold in total xp.
