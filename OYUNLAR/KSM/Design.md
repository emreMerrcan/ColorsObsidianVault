This game will be about sorting characters in a kill, sleep, marry fashion, but expanded on the idea. The game is made of 3 acts. Act 1 is the initial sorting stage. There the questions are mild and the default question is ANTAGONIZE, FRIEND, SLEEP. You sort 3 random characters to these choices. Antagonized ones get -1 favour and directly fall to the Losers list. Friends remain in the sorting group to be pulled again, and ger +1 favour point. If they reach 2 favour points, they rise to the lovers list. Slept with people go up to the Lovers list directly. 

# Act 1
Through act 1, the system sometimes pulls 3 characters with 1 favour and asks friend questions about them OR ask the default question of the act (antagonize, friend, sleep). (Sample shrinking can be done)

# Act 2
After all of the characters getting sorted at least once, Act 2 starts. Act 2 is where the game treats the two lists with separate questions. Default question for losers list is sorting for: KILL, ANTAGONIZE, FRIEND. Kill just kills that person and lowers them to the Dead tag, not to be randomly picked again. Antagonize and Friend give -1 and +1 favour respectively. -2 or less favour points make the character an enemy and leave the list. A character over +2 favour leaves the list unless they're FRIENDZONED, and rises to the lovers list.

During ACT 2, when a character falls into a tag, they are exempt from the default question pulling. Also during ACT 2, as the tags get filled, tag specific questions are being asked. Other then that, on act 2 a tagged character doesnt get pulled from
## Losers List:
-2 or less favour: Enemy. People antagonized, in conflict with or terribly disliked.
2+ favour: Friends. Broad, friendly social circle.
5+ favour: Besties. The closest aromantic or asexual social circle of a person.

On the lovers list, the default question is REJECT, SLEEP, MARRY. Rejecting someone gives -2 passion directly lowers them to the losers list. Sleeping with someone gives +2 passion. Marrying someone gives +3 passion.
## Lovers list:
Passion points. Anybody you sleep with gets 2, and marry gets 3. If you reject someone, they get -2. This is only to track the degree of which you picked them on repeat. Negative passion instantly drops the character to losers list.
# Act 3
Act 3 is triggered when there's no unlisted characters left. No default question in this act. The game concludes when only 1/3 of the characters are left outside of the conclusive tags. Then the game ends and shows a results screen. There every character is displayed from being most passionate with, to non, to then most favored then to the negative.

# TAGS:
DEAD: Killed characters, gone forever. Conclusive tag.
ENEMY: Antagonized characters, gone until act 3.
FRIEND: Overall friends and close social circle.
BESTIE: Still counts as friends. Has to have 5+ favour. Conclusive tag.
FRIENDZONE: People who fell from lovers list once and have high favour scores. Conclusive tag.
HOOKUP: Repeatedly slept with but not married people. Conclusive tag.
LOVER: Slept and married with people.
ONE-AND-ONLY: Still counts as a lover. 8 or more passion points. The most loved. Conclusive tag.
HAUNTING: Very situational, normally unachievable tag, which prevents the character to be killed or refused. Conclusive.

# THE QUESTION ENGINE:
Each act has its available question pool tailored for act specific purposes. Questions vary depending on ACT, TAGS and THRESHOLDS.

## Question Types:
- Cross-List questions: These questions pull characters that are close on sorting, but the end of the question can grately displace one of the participants. For example: Pull 3 from losers list, and ask to Kill, friend, sleep. The additional sleeping here makes even an unfavourable character to instantly jump to lovers list. Or same with asking Kill, sleep, marry on the lovers list, instantly removing them from the game.
- Overreaching questions: These questions pull characters from a wider range, including both losers and lovers. Since any question would be easy to answer (some are not very liked and some are obviously on that pulling), the questions have to get creative, and ask questions like, "Here's an enemy and a lover, kill both or sleep with both."
- Default questions: The default question per act, the full default question chart is, KILL, ANTAGONIZE, FRIEND, REJECT, SLEEP, MARRY, and on act 1, and then on for different lists on act 2 and 3 these questions are chosen differently.
- List specific questions. These are the eye of the game. Every tag gets their own question pool.
- Predeciments: These questions have no middle ground. They're eighter "Choose one or the other" or "Choose one among 3" and it asks you to completely discard the other characters form the tag or the list.

## Question pools for acts:
1- First act focuses solely to divide the characters into the lovers list or losers list, so aside from the default question, sometimes Tag specific questions are asked only for the FRIEND tag (in that act, 1 favour (unclassified) characters).
2- Second act focuses on tagging each character, but in the meantime spices things with Cross-list Questions, and Overreaching questions. Tag specific questions dominate this act with the default ones per list. 
3- At this stage all of act 2's questions are modified to be significantly harder to answer. The essential design is to make the player choose decisively among very close characters. Best among lovers, worst among enemies, the Predeciments dominate this act alongside with Tag specific (but modified) questions. This act is decisive and forces you to eliminate almost everyone.
## Question Methods:
Sample shrinking is for questions that only have 2 or less pull pool. If the question is antagonize, friend, sleep, its shrinked version for 2 samples is antagonize, sleep. Cutting the middle and forcing a sharp choice.

Predeciment modification: If possible, purposefully creating the most decisive and binary version of a question is deployed.
