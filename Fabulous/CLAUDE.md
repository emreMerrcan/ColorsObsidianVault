# Fabulous Diyarı: context for Claude

This is a summary of the world as the notes stand (read 2026-10-21). It's a map, not canon. When a detail matters, open the source note and quote it. The vault-wide rules in the root `CLAUDE.md` apply here too.

## What the world is

Fabulous (the user spells it both "Fabulous" and "Fabolous") is a mythic, layered fantasy world. Gods, "Originals", "Designers" and alien masters left ruins and rules behind, and mortal nations live in what's left. Reality isn't fixed: dreams, names, ideas and essence can rewrite it.

Core themes, from `Temalar.md`:
- **Çemberlerin Daimi:** parallel worlds in an unbroken chain (Kharan, Othra).
- **Öteye / Derinlere Yolculuk:** you can walk past the world's edge into other realities, and every object hides its own space of meaning.
- **Yankılar:** no act, wish or word goes to waste.
- Unwritten stubs: Tükenişler Tarihi, Eski Rüya, Meyal Ayıklık.

`Erikan` (from `Sözlük.md`) means any conscious being, down to almost animal level. Most of the regions are filed under `Erikan/`.

Both TTRPG campaigns take place in this world. Each has its own `CLAUDE.md`.
- `Naxal ve Düşünce İğnesi` is set in the city of **Xylorea**.
- `Emenos'un Gölgeli Sokakları` is set in **Emenos**, a city in southern Aldha'Menor.

The game `OYUNLAR/Asena Oyun` and the "Van'Asen Destanı" in the year plan draw on the Tayhun/Asena material.

## Ages, roughly in order

The notes give no single timeline. Pieced together:
1. **The beginning:** a storm of creation. The Kharan myth tells of a thousand-beaked death god who keeps the "lost word".
2. **Olia/Olea:** the ancient world and its hero-kings. **Originals (orijinaller)** walk the world.
3. **Second age:** the Tayhun clans unite under the Engin Kral.
4. **Age of the Designers (tasarımcılar).** The Adsız İdol, Vupelon, is born on the ocean floor.
5. **Arik'Xia / Xea rule:** the Golden Masters (Altın Efendiler) and Black Masters. Klix'Ether is the last great one. Also the Sekiz Gün Kıyımı.
6. **Beseloth's fall** starts an "age of madness". Karlopy hides inside one of their own gifts. Where this sits in the timeline is unclear.
7. **Aldha'Menor's calendar:** year 0 is Abhemon's uprising against the Xia, and it runs to about 1278.
8. **Büyük Buhran / Kıyamet:** Elruna returns, drinks every human feeling and becomes a mountain-swallowing darkness.

### Aldha'Menor essentials
- **Gün Dini:** the state religion. It deifies Abhemon (also called Amon), and you pray in sunlight three times a day. Its clergy are the Gadh' ranks: Nador > Nan > Raff / Kahar > Oria > Elon. It was founded by Phampamoa, Rhagon and Valerica. It's a war religion with harsh gender roles.
- **Night:** Kuvar is the god of night and owns all monsters. Abhemon's pact gave day to humans and night to monsters. Elruna is Kuvar's eldest daughter and the symbol of softness and hesitation. Her followers caused the Cinnet Dönemi.
- **Powers and factions:**
  - the Gaga Klanı, also called the crows / Kargalar, who seized power under Serbeni Alkan
  - the Kozmos knights, led by the undying Vanessa
  - İnlere Sinenler: the monstrous underground dwellers of Ghardonar's Damarları
  - Taykar, Sintra, Pattonia and Calibarrion as rivals
- **Late history:** Asırlar Savaşı with Taykar. Aghamenton's reforms and his murder. The child-king Ahmar. Cinnet Dönemi (4 years). Taykar takes Rhenes. The Kain Gündoğan period (1216–1278) ends in the Kıyamet.

## Magic and physics (`Büyü Sistemleri/`)

- **Düşlem:** re-dreaming the universe, with levels from small nudges up to rewriting the rules.
- **Dokulam:** binding ideas to objects.
- **Hepitem:** a being's nature lives in its bone marrow. **Hepitem boyama** paints one being's traits into another. Naxal's spine carries a Curcuna's hepitem.
- **Hak Lisan:** a language every thing understands. Names in it give power over what they name. It is Vellia'Ana's nature.
- **Marsim / Fıtrat Maskeleri:** masks that carry an absolute concept.
- **Özüt** (essence), from highest form down:
  - Marlon'un Kıvılcımı
  - Saf Özüt, which can only be stored in Taryen stones
  - Kara Özüt
  - Haron, a light-like wave (the sun is collapsed Haron)
  - Tiraç
  - Particles follow Ainex's rules.
- **Maddenin formları:** there are two drafts that don't agree.
  - Turkish, 6 forms: dengeli, uçuk, yüklü, çökmüş, yüksüz, boşluklar.
  - English, 5 forms, which adds "dark form".
- **Stubs:** Gece Gizi (Dilem), Havarilik Yetileri (12 gods' apostles), Kaçınılmaz Lehçe (rules of opposed gods), Yürek Hükmü (Elruna, shepherds/çobanlar), Biyomakine (empty).
- **Other terms:** Uvulon's sea, Whairion (the realm of minds, used heavily in the Naxal campaign), Pinaar / Şipaar Vakti (light phenomena).

## Known inconsistencies

Don't fix these silently. Point them out if they matter for a task.
- `Yöneticiler Külliyatı.md` and `İnsan Yurdu.md` both contain a list of rulers, and the two lists differ:
  - Teonomar vs. Teomer
  - "Soytarı Kral, Inuimudor II" vs. "Soytarı Kral, Kharan"
  - Teonomar II vs. Teomonar
  - different reign numbers
- **Period dates:**
  - Yükseliş Dönemi: 144–300 in one note, 144–600 in the other.
  - Duraksama Dönemi: 300–720 vs. 600–720.
- **The vizier:** Serbeni Alkan / Sergiu Albu / Sergeni Alban.
- **Spelling variants:** Phampamoa/Pampamoa/Phampamora, Illinar/Illanar, Calibarrion/Callibarrion, Kain/Khain, Xia/Xea.
- Klix'Ether is called the last Black Master, but `Gün Dini.md` calls the same figure "Ether, the last great Xia king".

## Writing in this world

- Tone: mythic and solemn, but the user also slips in crude jokes (Kraliçe İben, "Tatile çıkması dışında"). Keep both registers.
- The user writes lore as in-world history: chronicle voice, invented etymologies, myths with "denir ki".
- Most of the region folders are stubs. When asked to expand one, build from its "Ana Fikir" line and from what other notes already say about it, and mark it as AI text (rule 3).
